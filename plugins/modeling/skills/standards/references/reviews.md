# Fresh reviews: modeling

This reference can be wrong or out of date: verify what you rely on, as the [standards](../SKILL.md#verify-before-relying-on-them) require.

The method, the brief and what the agent must not be told are core's (`core:standards`, its fresh review). These are this domain's milestones.

| Milestone | The agent reads | It looks at |
|---|---|---|
| Models | models, its review | contact sheets with the game camera, `validate_mesh.py` reports, references |
| Characters | characters, its review | contact sheets with the game camera, `inspect_character.py` reports |
| Motion | animation, its motion craft | filmstrips, `inspect_animation.py` numbers, and frames of the running game when the engine has them |
